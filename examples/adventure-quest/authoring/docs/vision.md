# Vision

Adventure Quest is the smallest possible proof of both product pillars at once. Most
demos pick one — a clever agent, OR a file you own. This app insists on the pair: the
**agent is the dungeon master**, narrating every leg of a valley quest in warm
storybook prose, while the **world lives in the user's own SQLite file**, where the
pack and the travel journal are real tables the export button carries away whole.

The division of labour is the argument. The model tells the tale; the **app is the
referee** — destinations, loot and the sealed sun door come from its own scene graph,
and every delta the storyteller proposes is clamped hard, hearts never below one. A
child cannot be killed, robbed or stranded by a hallucination, because kid-safety is
structural, not prompted. And when the storyteller is away — no key configured, a
reply off-schema — a local guide narrates the same leg and says so plainly: the quest
never stalls on a missing key.

The emotional core is a kid's first save file that is really theirs. The glow mushroom
found in the whispering woods, the coins on the windy hill, the day the sun door
finally opened — written into a file on their own machine that no company holds. A
bedtime story you can export.
