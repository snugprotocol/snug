# Vision

A single-page Coinbase trading dashboard: real portfolio, real-time market prices, real-time
order book depth for a selected asset, and the ability to place real buy/sell orders —
market or limit — directly against the user's Coinbase account. An AI "copilot" persona
(Ledger) adds commentary: portfolio reviews, market takes, and reactions to orders just
placed. Should feel like a polished mini trading terminal, not a toy.

Requested by user: "build a dashboard which loads all my portfolio from Coinbase and lets
me execute trades too. The app should also show market prices from Coinbase in real time
and show Orderbooks in realtime for the selected crypto asset."
