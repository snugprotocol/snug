# Vision

Mint died the way every free finance dashboard dies: the product was the user. Ledger
is the counter-argument the Snug protocol exists to make — the most intimate dataset a
person has (every transaction they make) living in a file THEY own, analyzed by a brain
THEY chose, with recommendations that serve nobody else's affiliate table.

Ten steps ahead of Mint means three inversions, not ten features. **Custody**: the data
never leaves the user's file; there is no server to breach and no company to enshittify.
**Agency**: the analyst can be asked anything, plans against the user's own stated
goals, and shows its arithmetic — a projection you can check by hand beats a clever one
you must trust. **Consequence**: insight closes the loop into action — the money-leaks
radar does not just show a redundant subscription, it walks the user through cancelling
it and then PROVES the cancellation from the feed itself, tallying verified savings.

The time machine is the emotional core: one chart holding where you have been and every
future you are choosing between. The demo dataset is authored so that chart — and the
leaks it implies — lands before a single real bank is connected.
